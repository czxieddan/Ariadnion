## Complete terms

```text
The RNovModularDB dependencies included or configured by Ariadnion may be used
through this Ariadnion distribution chain only as dependencies of Ariadnion
itself or of a fork or extension that provides reasonable Ariadnion
functionality. For this restriction, reasonable Ariadnion functionality means
retaining and providing a material part of Ariadnion's modular service-gateway
or operations-platform functionality, rather than using Ariadnion merely as a
delivery vehicle for RNovModularDB. The dependencies must not be extracted,
repackaged, configured, or reused through this distribution chain for an
unrelated project, product, or service, or for standalone or general-purpose
database reuse. This condition restricts only the AHCL grant for this Ariadnion
distribution chain and does not grant, relicense, or dispose of any right in
RNovModularDB.
```
